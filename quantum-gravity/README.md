| [Correlation Actualization Theory](correlation-actualization-theory/README.md) | Yohtaro Soga | [v4 — 25 November 2026](correlation-actualization-theory/v4/README.md) |

CAT proposes observer-dependent gravitational layers, irreversible receipts, and constraints on subsequent propagation. Its finite examples retain specified physical inputs; they do not establish generic collapse or a completed all-rank theory.

# Correlation Actualization Theory

**A nonperturbative matrix proposal with observer-dependent layers**  
Yohtaro Soga

[Physics](../../README.md) → [Quantum gravity](../README.md) → CAT

CAT asks how a falling observer's continuation and a distant observer's accessible records can be described together. Ordinary matrix dynamics forms correlations; an additional physical postulate selects gravitational receipts irreversibly and changes the allowed inward and surface outputs. Its finite examples retain specified ranks, clocks, preparations, and ports.

## Research status and verification scope

Version 4 is an **unrefereed research preprint**. Exact inward closure concerns the specified constrained port; global closure requires every relevant port. The finite constructions do not establish generic natural collapse or a completed all-rank theory. A theory connecting ranks and clocks, natural preparation of the required ports, and evaporation-endpoint recovery remain open.

The [verification record](v4/VERIFICATION.md) distinguishes Version 3 executions of the selected numerical and Lean suites from Version 4 source-identity checks and a limited package check. The full selected suites were not rerun during the Version 4 review; the earlier execution evidence is inherited only where the relevant inputs are unchanged. Conditional mathematical and numerical checks do not establish that their physical inputs are realized in nature.

The technical reviews were AI-assisted development reviews, not journal peer review or independent human-expert acceptance. The [contribution statement](v4/paper/closing.tex) identifies the human author's concepts and research direction and Codex assistance with mathematical construction, literature searches, numerical and Lean code, checking, and drafting. Separate AI-generated technical reviews prompted corrections and clarifications.

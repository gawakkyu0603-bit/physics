# Correlation Actualization Theory

**A nonperturbative matrix proposal with observer-dependent layers**  
Yohtaro Soga

[Physics](../../README.md) · [Quantum gravity](../README.md) · [CAT reading site](https://gawakkyu0603-bit.github.io/physics/en/quantum-gravity/cat/index.html)

Correlation Actualization Theory (CAT) asks how an infalling observer's continuation and a distant observer's accessible records can be described together. Matrix dynamics forms correlations; an additional physical prescription selects gravitational receipts irreversibly and changes the allowed outputs at a later material contact. The paper develops specified finite constructions, quantum-recovery conditions, and proposed tests.

## Research scope

CAT is a research preprint. Its finite results depend on specified ranks, clocks, preparations, and physical ports. Closure of one constrained inward channel does not establish closure of every inward channel, and the prepared examples do not establish generic natural collapse. A quantum theory connecting rank and clock sectors, natural formation of the required ports, and recovery at the evaporation endpoint remain open. The readout protocol is a proposed experiment rather than an observed gravitational signal.

# Correlation Actualization Theory

**A nonperturbative matrix proposal with observer-dependent layers**  
Yohtaro Soga

[Physics](../../README.md) → [Quantum gravity](../README.md) → CAT

CAT asks how a falling observer’s continuation and a distant observer’s accessible records can be described together. Ordinary matrix dynamics forms correlations; an additional physical postulate selects gravitational receipts irreversibly and changes the allowed inward and surface outputs.

## Research status

This is an unrefereed preprint. Exact inward closure concerns the specified constrained port; global closure requires every relevant port. A theory connecting ranks and clocks, preparation in generic natural collapse, and evaporation-endpoint recovery remain open. The technical reviews were AI-assisted development reviews, not journal peer review.

The [contribution statement](v4/paper/closing.tex) identifies the human author’s concepts and research direction and Codex assistance with mathematical construction, literature, code, checking, and drafting.

## Scientific scope

The manuscript is a research preprint. Its finite constructions assume specified ranks, clocks, preparations, physical ports, and accessible carriers. Exact closure at a constrained inward port does not establish closure at every inward port. The common-source example uses a specified preparation; it is not a model of generic natural collapse. The proposed readout protocol has not been presented as an observed gravitational signal.

The paper identifies open questions concerning connections between rank and clock sectors, stable realistic matter and gravity, natural preparation of the required ports, and recovery at the evaporation endpoint. The three English guides explain these conditions along with the results.

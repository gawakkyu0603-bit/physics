## Editorial changes from Version 3

- The final measurement randomization now explicitly identifies the computational-basis
  Pauli X operation and explains the retained identity of the reference states.
- The inclusive interchange condition now points to the condition on maps for
  retained outcomes and subsequent control.
- The revised abstract, matrix-law discussion and conclusion distinguish the
  proposed law across ranks from the finite sectors actually calculated.
- A reading guide maps six physical questions to the manuscript and evidence.

The numerical inputs, reference results, programs, Lean sources and 91 numbered
research records remain unchanged. The [verification summary](https://github.com/gawakkyu0603-bit/physics/blob/cat-v4.0.0/quantum-gravity/correlation-actualization-theory/v4/VERIFICATION.md)
distinguishes the v3 full-suite reruns, v4 identity checks and v4 limited rerun.

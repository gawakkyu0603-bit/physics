# References for the bundled RIT data

The [official RIT catalog](https://ccrgpages.rit.edu/~RITCatalog/) identifies its CC BY-NC data terms and requests release-paper and simulation-specific citations. The following bibliography records the five release papers listed on the catalog as checked on 24 November 2026, and all distinct simulation-specific references for the five bundled waveforms.

## Catalog release papers

1. James Healy, Carlos O. Lousto, Yosef Zlochower, and Manuela Campanelli. **The RIT binary black hole simulations catalog.** *Classical and Quantum Gravity* **34**, 224001 (2017). [DOI: 10.1088/1361-6382/aa91b1](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6382/aa91b1); [arXiv:1703.03423](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.03423).
2. James Healy, Carlos O. Lousto, Jacob Lange, Richard O'Shaughnessy, Yosef Zlochower, and Manuela Campanelli. **Second RIT binary black hole simulations catalog and its application to gravitational waves parameter estimation.** *Physical Review D* **100**, 024021 (2019). [DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.100.024021](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.100.024021).
3. James Healy and Carlos O. Lousto. **Third RIT binary black hole simulations catalog.** *Physical Review D* **102**, 104018 (2020). [DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.102.104018](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.102.104018).
4. James Healy and Carlos O. Lousto. **Fourth RIT binary black hole simulations catalog: Extension to eccentric orbits.** *Physical Review D* **105**, 124010 (2022). [DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.105.124010](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.105.124010).
5. Giuseppe Ficarra, James Healy, and Carlos O. Lousto. **Fifth RIT catalog of binary black hole simulations: Multiple-resolution studies of eccentric orbits.** *Physical Review D* **114**, 064095 (2026). [DOI: 10.1103/p77n-6fxw](https://doi.org/10.1103/p77n-6fxw).

The review identified three issues that affect the physical interpretation of the second version. All three corrections and the three recommended clarifications are incorporated. [History law](parent-history.tex) · [Domain and overlap](supp-domains.tex) |
| V2-3. Can one control bound a false return? | An always-positive readout passes every positive control. The revised design measures both eigenstates, randomizes the terminal measurement and specifies an independently bounded error over all admissible conditional states, ancillary flags and ordinary inversion. The bounds \(0.6596\) and \(0.9702\) apply under these inputs. The old numbers require an independently established uniform envelope. [Design](supp-calibration.tex) |

[Computed probabilities](evidence/workspace/verification/cat-domain-calibration-results.json) |

The central proposal adds observer-dependent, gravitationally selected layers to ordinary matrix dynamics as a candidate framework for a theory of everything. Exact loss of the inward domain on the selected deficient history motivated the effective interaction. Its expected wide occurrence in natural collapse is a physical conjecture. The value 0.5376 is the probability of preparing that history in a particular flat device. Neither the echo calibration nor the finite code example establishes how often a star prepares it.

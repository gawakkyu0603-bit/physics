\section{Which constraints distinguish parent actions?}
A bare zero-dimensional matrix integral does not by itself give a trace-preserving real-time channel for a material observer. A BFSS Hamiltonian supplies a time variable directly; the ten-matrix representation supplies the same dynamics in the tested translation quotient and allows the internal-clock construction. These two descriptions with matched clocks cannot be ranked by comparing identical matrix elements. We chose the latter as parent because it expresses both calculations in one set of variables and treats the putative carrier of the layer as a further physical claim. The ordinary spatial T duality of a wrapped string is separate from passing to a clock quotient. A charge \(pn-qm\), read by a material reference \((p,q)\) on a flux pair \((m,n)\), is invariant when both transform by the same integral matrix of determinant one. That supplies a useful duality test on the receiver. It does not establish all eleven-dimensional supersymmetry or Lorentz Ward identities in the presence of receipt.

Several existing experiments constrain both representations. For a long-range unscreened universal scalar, the solar-system time delay gives \(\gamma-1=(2.1\pm2.3)\times10^{-5}\) (\href{https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01997}{Cassini}). A \(\gamma=1/8\) visible-matter branch is untenable. A \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.11761}{short-range torsion balance} constrains a gravitational-strength Yukawa range to less than \(38.6\,\mu{\rm m}\) at 95\% confidence. This bounds an actual force coupled to matter. Converting it to a length for a label would require a propagation law. \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05834}{GW170817 and GRB 170817A} constrain speed differences to roughly \(-3\times10^{-15}\) to \(7\times10^{-16}\) of light speed under the source-time assumptions. The empty-port ordinary tensor sector passes this comparison at tree level when its light cone is the matter cone. Residual propagation in a material medium still needs its own calculation.

\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21211}{Sodium nanoparticle matter-wave fringes} rule out always marking the transmitted paths at the reported masses; if every path is fully marked, unitary recombination cannot produce the observed fringes. They give no general bound based on mass alone for a port that requires actual gravitational absorption. \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09484}{Event Horizon Telescope images of Sagittarius A*} constrain particular reflective or thermally emitting surfaces. The existence of a closed inward link alone fixes neither visibility nor albedo. Ringdown \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08099}{from GW250114} and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19293}{catalogue tests of general relativity} constrain real waveforms and extra polarizations. The proposal supplies no substitute for computing a rotating collapse waveform with the same assumptions.

As an optional dark-sector test, suppose all the halo acceleration were a constant multiple of the scaled baryonic contribution within each galaxy. Applying the stated two-annulus selection to \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09251}{SPARC} gives a higher outer residual fraction in 56 of 68 galaxies; 42 retain it in the reported joint mass-to-light and velocity variations. A conserved positive uniform four-form instead gives outward acceleration and cannot itself make a galactic halo. In another branch the energy stored in a stable uniform elastic body strained by less than 0.1 is below \(1/7000\) of its rest mass under the subluminal sound-speed assumptions. Applying this budget to local baryons is inconsistent by a large margin with the inferred dark density \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.05334}{in the solar neighbourhood}. None of these exclusions requires the layer mechanism to produce dark matter or dark energy.

Macroscopic feasibility varies even for ordinary material. In a prepared thermal molecular example with Earth mass falling toward a \(10^6M_\odot\) hole, approximately 1,280 gravitons in the selected narrow cone can arrive before the matter. A 192-mode example for human and grain masses gives \(p_B<0.00911\); 128 modes in that preparation retain a floor of \(0.01303\). An actual charge condition, not the size of either number, supplies exact closure. The source and both compensation ports must exist in a given laboratory or stellar preparation before that criterion can be used.

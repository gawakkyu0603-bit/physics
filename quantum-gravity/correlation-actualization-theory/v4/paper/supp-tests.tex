\section{Falsification protocols and their domains}
A gravitational drive changes ordinary matter in the reversible reference theory too. A discriminating experiment must characterize the physical charge on separate trials and arrange the ordinary inverse on unmeasured trials. The predictions below cover different resolutions of a common instrument. Astronomical tests probe a proposed receiver only if its write fraction or reflectivity has been constrained independently.

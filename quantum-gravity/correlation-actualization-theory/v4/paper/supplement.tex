\section{How the interface was selected}
An exterior description that merely blocks a massive traveller behind a potential still contains inward states. Finite barriers have characteristic leakage, or an accessible low-energy sector. We followed this distinction from elementary matter--field exchange to propagating membrane probes.

\paragraph{Reversible exchange and conditional work.}
For \(H_{\rm int}=-Q^{kl}{\cal E}_{kl}/2\), both quadrupole and tide are ordinary physical variables. Under a prescribed weak classical tide the transition probability from material state \(|i\rangle\) to \(|j\rangle\) at first order in amplitude is
\[
 p_{i\to j}\simeq \frac{1}{4\hbar^2}\left|
 \int\dd t\, e^{i\omega_{ji}t}\sum_{k,l}\langle j|Q^{kl}|i\rangle{\cal E}_{kl}(t)\right|^2 .
\]
For a quantized tide the same matrix elements are contracted with the appropriate field correlation and recoil modes. Taking the coupling to zero removes this material source of writes. For a specified input and port the conditional matrix law fixes the Born distribution. A universal frequency for an arbitrary star would additionally require predicting the material states and their occupied crossings. The two ends receive opposite currents. In the continuum translation-invariant local realization, exchange takes the form \(\nabla_\mu T_i^{\mu\nu}=f_i^\nu,\ \sum_i f_i^\nu=0\) for the complete retained sectors when their stresses and interaction stress are included. The finite on-shell intertwining test in the article enforces the corresponding joint port balance.

\paragraph{Direct interaction or a mediator.}
For a quadratic degree of freedom with action \(xDx/2+x(J_1+J_2)\), the reduced functional has the cross term \(-J_1D^{-1}J_2\) and two self terms. They share a response spectrum. A direct coupling can reproduce the static cross term without sharing the same self-response, causal onset or energy budget. Three separately driven tidal inputs, recoil and short-time response can discriminate those cases. The adopted rule uses only the material inputs at the receiving junction. Conditional evolution of a particular observer can depend on a receipt while the unconditioned remote average stays fixed. Extending this consistency check from commuting local instruments to overlapping gravitational algebras remains a dynamical requirement.

\paragraph{Many receipts and a continuous set of layers.}
Let \(w(\xi)\dd\mu(\xi)\) be the Born measure of an occupied comparison and \(l_\psi(\xi)=\|T_\xi\psi\|^2\). Zero integrated cost requires \(l_\psi=0\) almost everywhere on positive-weight support. Increasing the number of independent receipts can push agreement probabilities very low. Correlated receipts with identical one-end marginals can nevertheless agree perfectly. A finite penalty \(H_\Delta=\Delta\bar\partial_L^\dagger\bar\partial_L\) likewise admits violations at finite energy. Constraining the physical section space gives an exact zero in the specified sector. The outgoing surface channel remains available.

\paragraph{Matching an ordinary exterior.}
With physical area \(4\pi R^2\) and only the two-dimensional metric and \(R\) dynamical, consider
\[
 S_{\rm sph}=\int\sqrt{-g_2}\,[F(R){\cal R}_2+Z(R)(\nabla R)^2+U(R)]\,\dd^2x .
\]
Demanding every neutral Schwarzschild exterior in this minimal two-derivative ansatz gives \(F=c_1R^2+c_0,\ Z=U=2c_1\). The topological and normalization freedoms are displayed. A null surface, a timelike surface and a surface coupled directly to an exterior tidal mode can have the same instantaneous area and different causal futures.

\section{Physical implementation and recovery}
\paragraph{A finite-rank check of the spherical interface.}
Represent a degree-\(n\) polarized sphere by homogeneous polynomials in two variables of degree \(n\). The two multiplication maps to degree \(n+1\) implement its homogeneous coordinates. A matrix link must intertwine both maps with their degree-\(m\) counterparts. The resulting shifted columns have zero extension at both ends. For \(n=2\) and \(m=1,2,3\) the finite nullities are \(0,1,2\). At matched degrees the diagonal coefficient fixes every column, independently of an unknown spectator state. The archive counts independent linked matrix coefficients for 143 degree pairs. The finite-column lemmas in \texttt{PreterminalMatching.lean} cover arbitrary natural \(m,n\) under the stated recurrences. They take the choice of physical polarization and intertwiners as input.

\paragraph{Which part of an unknown state can be read?}
For an initially independent material/field state \(\eta\), the effect on the payload is
\[
 G_z=\Tr_{\rm rec,field}\!\left[(1\otimes\eta^{1/2}){\cal U}_e^\dagger P_z{\cal U}_e
 (1\otimes\eta^{1/2})\right],\qquad p_z=\Tr(G_z\rho).
\]
For an initially correlated payload and environment one must evolve the joint input. Rotating the payload relative to a fixed receiver is physically different from rotating both. If there is just one Kraus operator per accessible flag, optimal recovery of a \(d\)-dimensional code maximally entangled with a reference gives \(F_e=d^{-2}\sum_z(\Tr\sqrt{G_z})^2\), with \(G_z\) restricted to the code. With unresolved information the full error-correction criterion in the article applies. The axial spin-one example can distinguish \(0\) from \(+1\) in part while leaving the two signs indistinguishable. The latter degeneracy protects their phase only when no other inaccessible mode carries the sign.

\paragraph{How much radiation is enough?}
The greybody luminosity is insensitive to phases relating different source states. Exact recovery tests the amplitudes, flags and residual matter jointly. On the massless particle content of Page's calculation, maximum entropies under the stated occupation bounds are \(0.25090,\ 0.02572,\ 1.33372\) nats in photons, gravitons and neutrinos, respectively, per nat of lost area entropy. For an initially capacity-filling code and a residual core limited by its area, photons alone cannot account for the lost capacity in this example. Massive thresholds, angular barriers and the Planck regime change the problem. The archive includes the numerical exterior transmission calculations. Selection of the parent action uses the part of evaporation where the semiclassical approximation can be checked.

\paragraph{Reversing more than one visit.}
Two later charges can neutralize each other while the ordinary inverse still fails to restore an earlier phase. A traveller who retains a material or gravitational record can distinguish visits to otherwise identical surroundings. If all accessible records are erased, their readings may coincide. When the same latent charge persists through \(k\) passages, the residual probability can be \(\mathbb E[p^k]\), even if the single-pass average is very small. Refreshing the charge independently gives \((\mathbb E[p])^k\) instead. This difference matters when applying a single-loop calculation to an entire time-travel scenario.

\section{A matrix realization of the measured geometry}
A flat sector has \([X^a,X^b]=i\theta^{ab}\id\) on a common invariant domain, with \(\theta^{01}=2,\theta^{23}=3\). Central double commutators vanish at infinite rank. An adjoint scalar sees a principal symbol proportional to \(\mathrm{diag}(4,-4,9,9)\), with the timelike entry second in these coordinates. A nonzero central commutator cannot hold exactly for finite ordinary matrices with finite trace.

For a curved sector take two canonical pairs \([U,V]=[Y,Z]=i\id\), with zero cross commutators, internal contraction \(\eta_{uv}=1\), and four constant matrices. Six vary:
\begin{gather*}
 X^u=U,\quad X^v=V+\phi,\quad X^y=Y,\quad X^z=Z,\quad
 X^4=f(U)Y,\quad X^5=f(U)Z,\\
 \phi=\tfrac12\,[a(U)(Y^2-Z^2)+b(U)(YZ+ZY)] .
\end{gather*}
The harmonic quadratic in \(Y,Z\) has zero transverse Laplacian. For \(x=(y,z)^T\), \(s=1+f^2\), \(B=ff'\), \(M=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\ b&-a\end{smallmatrix}\right)\) and the positive quarter-turn \(J\), the independent nonzero entries of the principal density are
\[
 H^{uv}=-1,\quad H^{ij}=s\delta^{ij},\quad
 H^{vi}=[J(M+B\id)x]_i,\quad H^{vv}=x^TM'x+f'^2 x^Tx,\quad \det H=-s^2.
\]
They give the article's wave metric after moving to its affine clock and a transported screen basis. In a Schwarzschild exterior the two transverse tidal eigenvalues along a nonradial null ray in a parallel frame are \(\pm3GM b_{\rm imp}^2/r^5\) for unit Killing energy. Matching those values tests the full tidal matrix on that ray rather than a single coordinate radius. It does not supply a global collapse solution. Likewise matching a common open past does not guarantee that two extended future metrics agree after their observers carry its clocks and orientation into the future.

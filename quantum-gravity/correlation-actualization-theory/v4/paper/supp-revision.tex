\section{One experiment from preparation to readout}\label{supp:joint}
The result filed as RES-0182 gives the following effective realization. It combines the ordinary tidal transition, the postulated occupied index readout, routing and two sequential emissions on a common preparation. It provides a test of compatibility among these operations. The material spectrum, coupling and source state specified below are inputs at this level.

\paragraph{Action and clock.}
We take \(\eta={\rm diag}(-,+,\ldots,+)\), \(X^0=-i\partial_\tau+a_0\),
\(D_\tau=\partial_\tau+i[a_0,\cdot]\) and a positive trace per clock period. Products of Grassmann matrices use graded cyclicity. With
\(\bar\Psi=-\Psi^T\Gamma^0\), \(\gamma_i=\Gamma^0\Gamma^i\) on the chiral components, the real-time reduction reads
\[
 g_1^2 L={\rm tr}\!\left[\tfrac12(D_\tau X_i)^2+\tfrac14[X_i,X_j]^2+
 \tfrac i2\psi_\alpha D_\tau\psi_\alpha
 -\tfrac12\psi^T\gamma_i[X_i,\psi]\right].
\]
Indeed \([X_0,\psi]=iD_\tau\psi\). Varying the connection gives
\(i[X_i,D_\tau X_i]+\sum_\alpha\psi_\alpha\psi_\alpha=0\). Multiplication of the full action by a common sign preserves this constraint's zero set, but changes the canonical momenta and the forward quantum phase. With \(\Pi_i=D_\tau X_i/g_1^2\), the singlet bosonic Hamiltonian is
\({\rm tr}(g_1^2\Pi_i^2/2-[X_i,X_j]^2/(4g_1^2))\).
The fermionic Hamiltonian adds the Yukawa term with positive displayed coefficient. Positivity here refers to the traced bosonic energy. For \(\tau=-i\tau_E\), \(a_0=i a_E\), one has \(D_\tau=iD_E\) and \(iS_L=-S_E\). The Euclidean bosonic terms are \((D_E X)^2/2-[X,X]^2/4\); the continued fermionic terms have signs \(+\psi^T D_E\psi/2+\psi^T\gamma_i[X_i,\psi]/2\). This is continuation of the reduced interval with analytically continued Grassmann variables. No unrestricted Euclidean matrix integral is required to define the state. At unit coupling \(X^1=\cos\tau\) gives \(+\pi/2\) in one \(2\pi\) period. For \(X^1=\cos\tau\,\sigma_x\), \(X^2=\sin\tau\,\sigma_z/2\), the period-averaged color traces yield \(1/2\) in real time and \(3/4\) for the Euclidean functional.

\paragraph{Ordinary emission, absorption and orientation.}
Use \(\hbar=c=1\) here and the source rest frame. Each emission reduces the source rest energy by \(2\omega\) and sends momenta \(+\omega\hat x,-\omega\hat x\). Define two orthogonal polarization amplitudes for the second pair by its normalized source matrix element \((\sqrt r,e^{i\phi}\sqrt{1-r})\). An oriented station with a quadrupole transition sees the vertex \(-\langle e|Q^{ij}|g\rangle\langle 0|{\cal E}_{ij}|1_\lambda\rangle/2\). Choose the tensor orthogonal to the cross polarization. On the two degenerate asymptotic states \(u\) (one bright graviton and a resting composite) and \(v\) (excited composite with momentum \(k\)), the scattering matrix is \((I+i\kappa\sigma_x)^{-1}(I-i\kappa\sigma_x)\). A third state \(d\) is the incident dark polarization. The reduced reactance, resonance and finite transfer time are effective assumptions. For \(M=3,\omega=4\) the initial total four-vector is \((7,\pm4,0,0)\) and the moving excited composite has \(M_*^2=33\). The compensating charge belongs to that composite. The empty and absorbed outputs are joint eigenstates of the same total four-vector and total charge. The outgoing projectors preserve their spectral blocks. For spacelike separated ports their extensions are \(K_z\otimes I\); the adjoint nonselective map fixes \(I\otimes B\) for every remote \(B\). A common gravitational dressing would require a new algebraic locality check.

In matrix units choose \(X_i=\ell\sigma_i\) in the selected three-plane. Its material orientation can be fixed by a measured ordered triad with nonzero \(-i{\rm tr}(X_1[X_2,X_3])=4\ell^3\). On one Pauli block the dimensionless probe spectrum at \(y=y_3/\ell\) is \(1-y,\ 1+y,\ -1\pm\sqrt{4+y^2}\); the opposite chiral block has opposite spectrum. Including the multiplicities in the sixteen-component spinor yields \(\nu=\tfrac12{\rm tr}_4\,{\rm sgn}\,D_+\). The two asymptotic probe positions \(2\) and \(1/2\) have indices \(0,1\). In this receiver the occupied transition takes the probe between those positions along the positive third axis, with a single zero at \(y=1\). Its assumed channel matrix element pays the gap and recoil. The spectrum establishes the signed index on that path. An uncontrolled star need not bind the excitation to that displacement. The terminal charge increases by one and the local compensator loses one. Simultaneously reversing coordinate conventions transports the triad, charges and complex polarization together; reversing only the material orientation relative to a fixed boundary changes the physical sign test.

\paragraph{Which histories were counted?}
The four port order is \(n_A,m_A,n_B,m_B\). Write \(t(1)=p,\ t(0)=1-p,\ d(j)=\delta_{j0}\). Outgoing states carrying the two source polarization patterns remain orthogonal at fixed clicks. Their exact joint law is
\[
 {\cal P}(i,j,k,l)=t(i)t(k)\{r\,t(j)d(l)+(1-r)d(j)t(l)\}.
\]
All registers start empty; the positive confirmation at A uses two absorbed packets. The wrong sign at B has probability \((1-r)p(1-p)\) and both B ends remain empty with probability \(r(1-p)+(1-r)(1-p)^2\). At \(p=0\) every register is empty. A possible four-bin causal graph has rest-frame flight time \(5\,\mu{\rm s}\) to each station, emission times \(0,2\,\mu{\rm s}\), local processing and a signal to the gate totalling at most \(1\,\mu{\rm s}\), and a routing decision at \(9\,\mu{\rm s}\) on the stationary gate clock. Both reports have arrived by \(8\,\mu{\rm s}\). For a freely recoiling absorber, its proper time during subsequent inertial motion is its lab time times \(M_* /(M+\omega)\). The graph posits localized time bins; an exactly monochromatic continuum wavepacket with compact support is not assumed. Source energy and recoil here are shell labels of the discrete effective modes. A Schwarzschild infaller's proper time and a stationary surface clock instead require the causal construction in the main text.

For this device \(1-b=(1-p)+(1-r)p^2\). The first term counts missed reference packets. The second counts trials where both ends absorb. Thus unit closure over the entire prepared ensemble requires \(p=r=1\). At the benchmark, the two open contributions are \(0.36\) and \(0.1024\). Conditioning on \(B_-\) establishes strict closure for that outcome but cannot establish typicality in a population of collapsing stars.

\paragraph{Which inward space is removed?}
The physical angular cross-section of the surface is the \(\mathbb{CP}^1\) of the boundary bundle. The orientation sphere of the material matrix probe is a different space. In the chosen low-energy band each tested species has a trivial internal bundle and the inward space is \(H^0({\cal O}(q))\otimes{\cal C}\). For a constant physical projector \(P\), the ordinary Hamiltonian must obey \((I-P)HP=0\). For a varying projector the condition is \((I-P)(H-i\hbar\dot P)P=0\). At discrete receipts an isometry transfers the incident state to the new inward-plus-surface domain. A smooth unitary transport of constant rank cannot on its own remove a dimension. The tested gate is block diagonal during free intervals. Its single declared inward port exhausts the code; no untested species is included. Smooth nonholomorphic sections exist even for \(q=-1\), and a species with internal twist \({\cal O}(1)\) has one holomorphic section there. Additional polarizations, energy bands and independent angular openings must all be checked before applying closure globally.

\paragraph{Recoil and complete histories of a qubit.}
A support of mass \(4\) and incident momentum \(-3\hat x\) changes to \(+3\hat x\) on the reflected branch. A payload of momentum \(+3\hat x\) then changes to \(-3\hat x\). These states have equal total energy and zero total spatial momentum; support energy is 5 in both directions. The swap on this joint shell conserves four-momentum. Acting on the payload alone would have a nonzero commutator with its momentum. For a matched configuration use the identity transmission; for a negative mismatch the exterior transfer is the whole accessible output of this junction. The opposite sign admits two inward modes; the tested input follows one of them. A fixture with other allowed directions would have to account for them.

At the boundary take an energy-degenerate \(d=2\) code, initially arbitrary and independent of the emitter. Each radiation carrier uses two orthogonal internal pair states of equal total energy and total momentum. The pair momenta are opposite in the emitting composite's rest frame and the core supplies their energy. Boosting this event preserves its four-vector balance. An inaccessible environmental register may distinguish the three carriers without learning the logical label. With \(a=1/2,\ e=2/3\), the successive branch weights are \(w=(a,(1-a)e,(1-a)(1-e))\). For each exterior history \(h\), the Kraus products reduce to \(\sqrt{{\cal P}(h)w_t}\,V_t\), where \(V_t|j\rangle=|t,j\rangle\) for the two pairs and core. The complete Gram entries are \({\cal P}(h)\sqrt{w_tw_u}\,\delta_{tu} I_2\). The receiver measures the carrier sector and inverts its embedding. On an inward history the only accessible output is instead \(|h,\perp\rangle\); hidden errors \(|h,\perp\rangle\langle j|\) have non-scalar cross terms for \(j\ne j'\). An erased half of a Bell pair has overlap \(1/4\) with that Bell state for any replacement qubit. These history products give the fidelities in the article, including the erasure from losing the core. They evaluate a single payload relative to a specified receiver. The comparable data are the source preparation and the charge outcomes.

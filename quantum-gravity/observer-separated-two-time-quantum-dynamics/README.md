# Observer-Separated Two-Time Quantum Dynamics

**Local Horizon Obstructions, Cross-Perspective Consistency, and Finite-Dimensional Evaporation Bounds**

Yohtaro Soga · 26 November 2026

An observer-indexed quantum framework separates reversible clock-time evolution from auxiliary-time dephasing, with conditional results on energy conservation, local horizon incompatibility, quantum recovery, entropy, and information bottlenecks.

## Mathematical results

| Topic | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Energy moments | Conservation of the first two energy moments is equivalent to commuting jumps, and implies two-time integrability. |
| Horizon locality | Restriction to the exterior algebra can obstruct simultaneous conservation, even for globally commuting Hamiltonians. |
| Recovery | A joint extension satisfies the sharp linear sum bound; separate reversible channels have their own inverse decoders. |
| Spectral pinching | Hermitian jumps decrease purity; commuting sector-separating jumps give explicit block decay. |
| Dimension bottleneck | Encoding and decoding through dimension $d_f$ have entanglement fidelity at most $\min(1,d_f^2/d_C^2)$. |

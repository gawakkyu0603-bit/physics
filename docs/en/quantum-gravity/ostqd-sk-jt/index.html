Scope shared by all introductions

The paper builds on the preceding finite-dimensional OSTQD study by relaxing strict subsystem energy conservation for reduced open systems. The guides cover SK normalization, KMS covariance and its time-dependent limits, Ward identities and JT fluctuation closures, bosonic and chiral-horizon diagnostics, per-port thermal locking, greybody scattering, and separated information benchmarks.

The focused analytical results are conditional: a specified free chiral-boson smooth-band defect and the frozen-coefficient JT cumulant closure. A positive global continuum state, a microscopic rate, observer-separated outcome instruments and a unified evaporation endpoint remain open.

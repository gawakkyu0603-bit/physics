TL;DR

An observer-indexed quantum framework separates reversible clock-time evolution from auxiliary-time dephasing, with conditional results on energy conservation, local horizon incompatibility, quantum recovery, entropy, and information bottlenecks.

Three English guides cover the same research at different reading levels, including its assumptions, results, and open questions.

Scope shared by all introductions

All guides cover the two-time framework, the five conditional results, the two-window evaporation example, the independent matrix-spectrum benchmark, and open questions.

The mathematical statements concern specified finite-dimensional systems. No continuum gravitational dephasing, observed firewall, or experimentally measured departure from standard quantum mechanics is asserted.

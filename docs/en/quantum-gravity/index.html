Quantum gravity

Research on quantum theory, gravitation, spacetime, and quantum information.

Papers

  • Paper 1 — Observer-Separated Two-Time Quantum Dynamics

    An observer-indexed finite-dimensional framework separates reversible clock evolution from non-selective auxiliary dissipation, with conditional energy-moment, local horizon, joint-channel, shared-record, purity and unassisted compression results.

  • Paper 2 — Consistency Audits of Observer-Separated Open Dynamics in 2D Dilaton Gravity

    A thermal open-system continuation of OSTQD: KMS covariance and uniform-contraction tracking, frozen-coefficient JT cumulants and an exactly reversible energy jump model, and the constructive Mellin positive-type extension of a free chiral horizon defect.

  • Paper 3 — Observer-Separated Selective Instruments, Born-Weighted Branching, and Non-Isometric Pullback Defects in Type II Gravitational Algebras

    The third OSTQD study develops selective instruments on causal observer contexts, conditional steering obstructions and Born-weighted branch records, a semifinite entropy split, sharp non-isometric pullback bounds and causal/linear controls on branch coupling.

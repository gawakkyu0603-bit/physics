Scope shared by all introductions

The guides cover observer-poset instruments, conditional state change, Local Friendliness, signed global couplings, contextual Born records, semifinite entropy, sharp polar-isometric bounds, causal sequential emission and five distinct branch-coupling tests.

Finite clock and evaporation examples are not continuum gravitational constructions. The semifinite theorem is analytical; relativistic gluing, gravitational crossed-product instruments, a microscopic branch-transition rate and autonomous matrix evaporation remain open.

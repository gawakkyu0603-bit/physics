Scope shared by all introductions

Each introduction covers the motivation and action choice; receipts, histories and route closure; common preparation and quantum recovery; material clocks; spacetime and vacua; matter and scales; experiments, astronomy and cosmology; verification and open problems. General readers receive examples and intuition, students the key equations, and experts the definitions, conditions and quantitative comparisons.

Diagrams identify conditional models and explanatory schematics. The text distinguishes mathematical results from mechanisms that remain unconfirmed in nature.

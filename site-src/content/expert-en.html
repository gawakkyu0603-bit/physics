TL;DR

  • Proposal: add an irreversible gravitational receipt and charge-controlled changes of propagation ports to ordinary matrix dynamics.
  • Established scope: a relative degree of −1 gives exactly zero inward dimension at the specified spherical port. In a finite common-source example, A's positive receipt and B's closure coexist in 19.6608% of trials.
  • Tests: compare quantum recovery, return after reversible operations, and astronomical phases, distances and forces under stated receipt and error conditions. The 900-trial return protocol is a proposed experiment.
  • Wider programme: examine clocks, emergent geometry, vacua, matter, radii and connections to other gravity theories. Joint realization in natural collapse, probabilities across ranks, a realistic stable vacuum and the evaporation endpoint remain open.

Expert introduction · Quantum information, matrix models and gravity

What physical law does CAT add?

Reversible matrix dynamics, an irreversible gravitational record, and a change of propagation routes controlled by that record. This introduction reads Version 4 of Correlation Actualization Theory (CAT) through these three operations and the conditions under which they can be tested.

Source: Yohtaro Soga, Version 4, 25 November 2026. Research preprint without external referee acceptance.

From different descriptions to different physical outputs

An observer A falling into a black hole and an observer B remaining far away cannot acquire the same records. In general relativity, A crosses the horizon in finite proper time. The redshift of signals received by B does not, by itself, establish that A was destroyed at the horizon. CAT adds a proposed law that permits A's inward continuation while removing the inward channel from B's present physical description, whose outputs then occupy the surface and exterior.

Conditioning on B having less information does not accomplish that change. The paper calls a material junction capable of receiving the relevant currents a “port.” It specifies the charge that reaches the port before its deadline and the output space permitted by that charge. Observers comparing the same delivered record in a common causal region must agree on its probabilities. A single common spacetime is not assumed to extend to endpoints that cannot be compared. Nor does the proposal simultaneously clone one unknown quantum state into an A copy and a B copy. Paper, pp. 1–2; supplement, p. 7

Add history selection to a common matrix action

The ordinary reversible sector uses IKKT-type interactions. There are ten Hermitian matrices \(X^a\) and a Majorana–Weyl spinor \(\Psi\). With internal contraction \(\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-,+,\ldots,+)\) and forward weight \(e^{+iS_0}\), the convention is

\[ S_0=\frac{1}{g_0^2}\operatorname{Tr}\!\left( \frac14[X^a,X^b][X_a,X_b] +\frac12\bar\Psi\Gamma^a[X_a,\Psi]\right). \]

Ten counts the matrix components; it is distinct from the size \(N\) of each matrix. A chosen clock sector reduces the ordinary action to matrix quantum mechanics, with a positive kinetic term for a physical scalar. The internal signature, clock and preparation are inputs to the construction. “Nonperturbative” here means using the full coupling dependence of the specified scattering blocks. It does not mean that a quantum theory connecting all ranks has already been constructed. Paper, pp. 4–7

At contact \(e\), let \(\mathcal U_e\) describe ordinary evolution, \(P_{e,z}\) project onto the deposited charge, and \(B_{e,z}\) be an isometry that includes the physical record and recoil. The outcome \(z\) includes an empty receipt. If \(R_O\) selects the part of the record delivered to observer O, the instrument on a fixed contact network has the form

\[ \begin{aligned} K_{e,z}&=B_{e,z}P_{e,z}\mathcal U_e, &\sum_zK_{e,z}^{\dagger}K_{e,z}&=\mathbf1,\\ L_{e,z}&=\mathbb W_eK_{e,z}, &L_{\boldsymbol z}&=L_{e_s,z_s}\cdots L_{e_1,z_1},\\ \mathcal I_r^O(\rho)&=\sum_{R_O(\boldsymbol z)=r} L_{\boldsymbol z}\rho L_{\boldsymbol z}^{\dagger}. \end{aligned} \]

The trace gives the Born weight; normalization gives the conditional state. When all outcomes are discarded, every observer obtains the same total channel on this fixed network. CAT's additional physical assumption is that a gravitationally received charge sector is selected irreversibly and that the recorded charge controls a later physical port. Ordinary environmental decoherence alone does not supply this fundamental irreversibility. Conservation laws including recoil and compensating charge impose further conditions: trace preservation alone does not establish conservation of energy and momentum. Paper, pp. 5–6

What identifies a gravitational receipt?

The ordinary tidal interaction \(H_{\rm tide}=-Q^{ij}\mathcal E_{ij}/2\) couples a material quadrupole to local curvature. Absorption requires real energy and angular-momentum exchange. In the matrix description, the spectrum of a spinor probe in a physically oriented material three-plane defines a signed index across a gap. Its change is associated with received charge. A reversible level crossing and irreversible selection following an occupied absorption are separate stages.

The supplement's Pauli-matrix receiver gives index 0→1 on a probe path from dimensionless position 2 to 1/2. A material triad fixes orientation; the receiving end's +1 is paired with −1 at a compensator. That a prepared transition drives this displacement is an input, not a demonstration that an arbitrary star follows the same path. Empty intervals without a gravitational receipt retain ordinary unitary evolution. Supplement, pp. 4–6

Conservation and causal order require more than completeness

The doubled history representation assigns \(e^{iS_0[X_+]-iS_0[X_-]}\) to ordinary intervals and inserts \(\sum_zK_z\otimes\overline K_z\) at receipts. Each receipt map must also intertwine the spectral blocks of conserved total generators. In the finite example, separated ports act on distinct tensor factors, so a nonselective local operation fixes the entire remote observable algebra. Extension to overlapping algebras with general gravitational dressing remains a further problem.

Exchanging the clock order of independent contacts requires agreement for every retained pair of outcomes and subsequent control, not merely commutation after outcomes are discarded. The supplement gives a counterexample: measure one qubit in Z or X and reset it to the maximally mixed state. The inclusive maps agree, but from \(|0\rangle\), the probability of two positive records is 1/2 in one order and 1/4 in the other. Version 4 clarifies this outcome-wise order condition. Supplement, pp. 6–7

Small transmission and an absent state space are different

At a common input port, if \(\rho_B\geq\lambda\rho_A\) with \(\lambda>0\), every positive inward effect satisfies \(p_B\geq\lambda p_A\). Broadening an ensemble that still contains A's state, or applying finite damping, cannot make B's probability exactly zero. The construction therefore selects the allowed subspace itself using comparison maps \(T_e\) at occupied contacts:

\[ \begin{aligned} C_{\boldsymbol n}&=\sum_e n_eT_e^{\dagger}T_e,\\ P_{\boldsymbol n}&=\mathbf1_{\{0\}}(C_{\boldsymbol n}),\\ W_{\boldsymbol n}&=P_{\boldsymbol n}\otimes\mathbf1 +(\mathbf1-P_{\boldsymbol n})\otimes S. \end{aligned} \]

Here \(n_e\) counts occupied contacts, and \(S\) swaps the degenerate inward and surface routes. An input can continue inwards only if it lies in the kernel of every occupied comparison. This differs from merely increasing a coupling to suppress transmission.

In the concrete spherical model, the two material ends have degrees \(n,m\), and the inward field is constrained to be a holomorphic section of \(L=\mathcal O(m-n)\). Standard line-bundle mathematics gives

\[ N_{\mathrm{in}} =\dim H^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(m-n)) =\max(1+m-n,0). \]

For \(m-n=0\), one spatial mode can carry an arbitrary internal quantum state. For \(-1\), there is no inward mode; for \(+1\), there are two. This mathematical count must be distinguished from the physical hypothesis that all relevant matter components obey the constraint. Another species or another port can reopen transmission. A wholly exterior B-description requires closure of every accessible inward port. Paper, pp. 3–6

The finite-matrix version counts the support of columns intertwining two coordinate multiplication maps. For \(n=2\), the cases \(m=1,2,3\) have nullities 0, 1 and 2. Independent counts for 143 degree pairs accompany conditional Lean lemmas for arbitrary natural degrees. Nonholomorphic sections still exist at \(m-n=-1\), however, and an internal species twist \(\mathcal O(1)\) restores a holomorphic section there. The physical polarization restriction is a substantive assumption.

Why a mediator or finite barrier is insufficient

Eliminating a Gaussian mediator gives a cross term \(-J_1D^{-1}J_2\) between material sources. A direct interaction can reproduce its static force without reproducing its self-response, causal onset or recoil. For continuously many layers, a positive weighted comparison cost vanishes only when the comparison vanishes almost everywhere on its occupied support. Increasing the number of layers does not exclude perfectly correlated receipts.

A finite energetic penalty merely moves violating states to higher energy. Preserving a physical subspace during free evolution requires \((I-P)HP=0\) for fixed \(P\), or \((I-P)(H-i\hbar\dot P)P=0\) for varying \(P\). Smooth unitary transport of constant rank cannot remove a dimension. At a receipt, the construction instead uses an isometry into the new inward-plus-surface domain. Supplement, pp. 2–3, p. 6

Matching ordinary exterior gravity is another independent requirement. In a minimal two-derivative action varying only the two-dimensional metric and spherical radius, demanding every neutral Schwarzschild exterior fixes the curvature coefficient to \(F(R)=c_1R^2+c_0\) and the radius kinetic and potential coefficients to \(Z=U=2c_1\). This is a matching condition within the restricted ansatz, retaining normalization and topological freedoms.

An exact zero in the inward-mode count does not apply to a star unless collapsing matter prepares the required receipts. Tidal excitation, retarded arrival, absorption at both ends and the time to the subsequent contact must be computed from one preparation. A and B do not use interchangeable clocks.

Compute both receipts from one preparation

A finite example sends two pairs of gravitational packets to two stations whose registers are initially empty. The second pair is in a correlated polarization state in which just one side is bright; \(r\) denotes the corresponding source weight. The resonant absorption probability of a bright packet is determined by a reactance \(\kappa\). Let \(A_+\) mean that both of A's ends actually absorbed, and let \(B_-\) mean that B's relative charge is \(-1\). Then

\[ \begin{aligned} p&=\frac{4\kappa^2}{(1+\kappa^2)^2},\\ \Pr(A_+)&=rp^2,\\ b=\Pr(B_-)&=rp+(1-r)p(1-p),\\ \Pr(A_+,B_-)&=rp^3. \end{aligned} \]

At \(r=3/4\) and \(p=0.64\), A has a positively confirmed receipt in 30.72% of trials, B closes in 53.76%, and both occur in 19.6608%. The inward dimension is exactly zero within the selected B-closed sector, while the fraction of trials entering that sector is 53.76%. Equality of two empty registers does not count as positive confirmation of A's receipt. Paper, pp. 8–9

The unknown qubit is routed once in each observer's description. On B's closed histories, the example specifies an isometric channel into a photon pair, a graviton pair or a residual core with weights \(1/2,1/3,1/6\). Recovery is exact when all carriers remain accessible. Including the erasure flags on inward histories, the fidelity for a maximally entangled input is \(F_e^B=b+(1-b)/4=0.6532\). This flat-spacetime apparatus contains neither collapsing matter nor a horizon. Its 53.76% closure fraction is not an astrophysical closure rate.

The open-history weight \(1-b=0.4624\) consists of missed reference absorption, 0.36, and absorption at both ends, 0.1024. Closing every trial in this apparatus requires \(p=r=1\). Even at \(p=1,r=3/4\), the whole-history fidelity is \(13/16\); discarding the core reduces it to \(23/32\). Exact conditional recovery and recovery without postselection remain distinct.

The apparatus includes momentum balance. In the supplement, a support of mass 4 exchanges momentum of magnitude 3 with the payload, preserving support energy 5 and zero total momentum across reflection. Reflecting the payload alone would violate that balance. A discrete-mode schedule with emissions at 0 and 2 μs, flight time 5 μs, processing within 1 μs and a gate decision at 9 μs permits timely reception. These numbers do not replace the two proper-clock calculations in Schwarzschild geometry. Supplement, pp. 5–7

Recovery tests a channel, not just radiated power

Entangle the unknown input with an untouched reference and ask whether that entanglement can be preserved. With accessible classical flag \(z\), inaccessible degree of freedom \(\alpha\), and code projector \(P\), exact recovery for the specified channel requires

\[ P K_{z\alpha}^{\dagger}K_{z\beta}P =c_{\alpha\beta}^{(z)}P \quad\text{for every }z,\alpha,\beta. \]

This is the flagged quantum-error-correction condition. Accurate charge readout can still lose phase if the receiver distinguishes two code components. The axial spin-1 receiver writes with probabilities \(1/2,15/16,1/2\) for \(m=-1,0,+1\). A qubit encoded in \(0,+1\) has optimal corrected fidelity \(F_e=0.930715\), while a degeneracy that does not distinguish the two signs may protect a different code. A chiral receiver can instead read only the bright sign.

Hawking greybody factors and temperature constrain intensities but do not determine the Gram matrix of amplitudes for different inputs. Three sign choices at the same intensities give emission from an adjacent-level difference equal to \(7.47\times10^{-8}\), 0.00974 and 1.00338 times the mean in the specified window. Reproducing the spectrum therefore does not guarantee quantum recovery.

Under the supplement's occupation bounds, maximum entropies per nat of lost area entropy are 0.25090 nats in photons, 0.02572 in gravitons and 1.33372 in neutrinos. With an initially capacity-filling code and an area-limited residual core, photons alone are insufficient in that example. This motivates including every accessible species, residual matter and flag. Massive thresholds and the quantum endpoint require reconsidering the semiclassical estimate. Paper, pp. 7–9; supplement, p. 3

Read spacetime from material clocks and waves

A finite clock without external coordinate time

Fix two material oscillators' total occupation to \(N_c\); one exchanged unit advances the reading \(q=n_b\), and the source starts at \(q=0\). Here \(N_c\) denotes clock capacity, avoiding confusion with matrix size. Using the polar part \(V\) of a shift that vanishes at the endpoint and local receipt maps gives

\[ \begin{aligned} T_z&=\sum_{q=0}^{N_c-1}V|q\rangle\langle q|\otimes K_{z|q},\\ \Phi(H)&=\sum_zT_zHT_z^{\dagger},\qquad \Omega=\rho_{\rm source}+\Phi(\Omega) =\sum_{j=0}^{N_c}\Phi^j(\rho_{\rm source}). \end{aligned} \]

The finite chain terminates, and normalization on a clock slice gives its conditional state. \(\operatorname{Tr}\Omega\) counts visited slices rather than representing a unit-normalized total probability. No input coordinate time or four-manifold is required, but an algebra, internal signature, monotone clock sector and source are required. A physical measure including clocks that turn, split or run out remains unavailable.

Separate a propagation cone from Einstein's equations

A small Hermitian scalar displacement of an unused spatial matrix obtains its real-time sign from the two factors of \(i\) in semiclassical commutators. In the clock quotient, \(\phi=\cos\tau\) at unit coupling gives action \(+\pi/2\) over one period. This checks the physical sign rather than inferring an energy convention from the wave cone alone.

\[ \begin{aligned} H^{\mu\nu}&=\rho_\theta\theta^{\mu\alpha}\theta^{\nu\beta} \partial_\alpha x^a\partial_\beta x_a,\\ g_{\mu\nu}&=\sqrt{|\det H|}\,(H^{-1})_{\mu\nu}\qquad(d=4). \end{aligned} \]

Here \(H\) is the principal density of the wave operator, \(\theta\) the Poisson tensor, and \(\rho_\theta\) the trace density. Extracting a metric in a nondegenerate four-dimensional sector does not make arbitrary matrix data satisfy Einstein's equations. The supplement's flat example has principal symbol proportional to \(\operatorname{diag}(4,-4,9,9)\), with its timelike component in the second coordinate. A nonzero central commutator also cannot hold exactly for ordinary finite matrices with finite trace; that construction uses an infinite-rank common domain.

For a curved ordinary matrix family, set \(s=1+f^2\) and \(dt=s\,du\). Then

\[ \begin{aligned} ds^2&=-2\,dt\,dv+d\boldsymbol X^Td\boldsymbol X +\boldsymbol X^TK(t)\boldsymbol X\,dt^2,\\ R_{titj}&=-K_{ij},\qquad \tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}K=\frac{a^2+b^2}{s^2}-\frac{f'^2}{s}. \end{aligned} \]

Primes denote \(u\) derivatives. The condition \(a^2+b^2=sf'^2\) selects a vacuum Einstein wave and allows both polarizations on a finite interval. In a general matter background, matrix variation gives a projected equation \((\delta g/\delta A)^*\mathcal E=0\). Obtaining all of \(\mathcal E=0\) requires the missing metric variations or constraints, together with a common cone for actual species. Paper, pp. 9–10; supplement, pp. 3–4, 9

Beyond a singularity, shared spacetime and different vacua

The same incoming tidal data need not determine later material phases or excitations uniquely. A rotating six-matrix example has a volume bound \(V\geq|L|/E^{1/4}\) from angular momentum. At normalized speed \(h=1/2\), integrated null convergence over a cycle is −1.07718, exhibiting defocusing at turnaround. The fifth angular mode changes stability at \(h=\sqrt5/4\). Starting in its selected ground state gives excitation probabilities of 1.81% after one cycle and 14.45% after three. Smooth geometric continuation is not equivalent to erasing material memory or establishing complete stability.

Calling this a generic vacuum transition would require an amplitude between inequivalent vacuum sectors. The paper supplies no such amplitude for general collapse or a hot early universe. B's closed model removes its inward domain at an earlier contact, so it poses a different question from A's high-curvature continuation.

A common-spacetime approximation requires consistent transport of clock ratios, light-travel times, polarizations and orientations on overlaps, including triple overlaps. Two future wave geometries can agree on return times along perpendicular arms but give 0.33301 and 0.30801 on a 45-degree arm; a common value then needs tolerance at least 0.01250. Discarding orientation can allow a reflection to identify them. Comparability depends on the retained data.

The discussion of exotic \(\mathbb R^4\) likewise does not assign a different smoothing to each observer. Local embeddings and restrictions of curved solutions, complete global metrics and global hyperbolicity impose separate conditions. Many material observers can occupy one ordinary vacuum; inequivalent vacua can instead change a tidal invariant accessible to a suitable probe. Paper, pp. 10–11

A four-dimensional vector plus six scalars gives the same count. Four-dimensional spacetime is treated as an effective dimensionality resolved by material probes in particular states.

Vacuum counts, radius stabilization and realistic matter

Counting classical matrices, inequivalent geometries, quantized fluxes and stable quantum ground states answers different questions. In the specified square-torus orientifold with fixed coupling and tadpole bound, 48,055,489 even imaginary-self-dual flux configurations reduce to 20,597 under a primitive \((2,1)\) condition and to 115 orbits after the finite lattice and duality quotient. Forty-nine linearly fix ten complex shape/coupling variables; 41 saturate the O3 charge. These are not counts of all CAT vacua.

If gravitational comparisons depend only on relative scale, a uniform shift of every log radius leaves the kernel unchanged. That direction becomes observable when atomic masses depend on the common radius. Comparing ordinary CAT, IKKT and static BFSS on the same static six-dimensional background gives cancellation of the specified periodic-spinor one-loop contribution. For a free positive-energy excitation with zero external three-momentum, \(E^2=X+Y\) and \(X,Y\geq0\),

\[ \frac{-\partial_sE}{E}=3+\frac{3Y}{4(X+Y)}. \]

Every constituent in this class pushes in the same direction, so positive Born mixing cannot supply a restoring force. Localized bound states or a receipt coupling the paths may contribute differently, but require actual matrix elements. The outer size of an object and the scale of its internal space are also distinct degrees of freedom.

Separate irreversible loss from a false return

If the ordinary apparatus and its environment can be fully inverted, a unitary model returns to the initial state. Starting from a pure initial state, let an occupied CAT contact select orthogonal charge groups with weights \(p_z\). After undoing the isometries within each sector and projecting all registers onto their original state, the return probability is \(\sum_zp_z^2\). A different experiment reads only a logical qubit and has ideal return probability \(1-p/2\). Both positive and negative references are needed to exclude a detector that reports success on every input.

Immediately before a stable terminal measurement, apply either \(I\) or the computational-basis Pauli \(X\) with equal probability. Since \(X|\pm\rangle=\pm|\pm\rangle\), this does not interchange the return-basis references. It averages away the measurement effect's off-diagonal terms in that basis:

\[ \frac{E+XEX}{2}=aP_++b_{\mathrm{ro}}P_-,\qquad a=\Pr(+\mid+),\quad b_{\mathrm{ro}}=\Pr(+\mid-). \]

The false-positive rate is written \(b_{\mathrm{ro}}\) here to distinguish it from the preceding closure fraction. The supplement's design targets absorption \(p=8/9\) and uses 900 trials: 200 for absorption, 300 positive references, 300 negative references, and 100 signal trials. It assumes an independently established error envelope \(\Delta\leq0.01\) covering all admitted states and environment flags, and dark-click probability \(d\leq0.01\). Conditional on passing calibration, the bounds are

\[ \begin{aligned} \lambda_{\mathrm{CAT}}&\leq1-\tfrac12p_L(1-e)+\Delta \simeq0.659582,\\ \lambda_{\mathrm{ordinary}}&\geq1-e-\Delta \simeq0.970227,\\ p_L&\simeq0.714974,\qquad e\simeq0.0197734. \end{aligned} \]

In the 100 signal trials, at least 88 returns reject the specified CAT model; at most 85 reject the ordinary-inversion model with the stated error bound. Counts of 86–87 remain undecided. A failed calibration also produces no claim. For the whole procedure, the calibration error budget of 0.01 must be added to the small tail probability of the signal block alone. Independent, stationary trials and the two hardware bounds remain separate assumptions. These are prospective operating conditions, not results achieved by an apparatus. Paper, p. 13; supplement, pp. 7–8

The 900 trials constitute one prespecified attempt, without automatic retry after failed calibration. At the design point \(a=0.999,b_{\mathrm{ro}}=0.001\), detection efficiency one, \(d=0.01\) and zero residual transfer error, all three calibration blocks pass with probability about 0.54861. Substituting the preceding \(p=0.64\) example makes the absorption-cut pass probability about \(1.04\times10^{-6}\), even at optimal detection efficiency. A large discriminating signal and a qualified apparatus are separate achievements.

Define “returning to the past” through retained records

Reversing the surroundings while a traveller keeps a memory makes the later visit distinguishable even if the clock repeats its reading. Including the traveller and all reversible memories in the inverse still leaves a return deficit if CAT receipt selection occurred. One four-alternative example has inward probability one but complete return only 29.82%. Later charge neutralization need not restore lost phase.

Repeated passages with the same latent charge can give \(\mathbb E[p^k]\), whereas independent refresh gives \((\mathbb E[p])^k\). This record-based analysis does not solve the general classification of closed timelike curves. Paper, p. 13; supplement, p. 3

From laboratories to cosmology: which observation excludes which branch?

It is insufficient that gravity participates in an experiment. Charge must be calibrated on separate trials, while ordinary leakage, environment and recoil are controlled on signal trials. Astronomy requires an independently constrained distinguishing-write fraction or reflectivity. The following catalogue retains the proposed tests and their domains rather than combining conditions from different vacua into one universal prediction.

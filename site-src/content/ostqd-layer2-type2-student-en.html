Observer contexts and quantum instruments (§§1–2)

Let A_O denote an observer's accessible algebra and c a measurement context on a finite causal poset. A finite-dimensional instrument consists of CP/TNI maps I_{O,c,x} whose sum is trace preserving. The outcome weight is p_x=τ(I_x(ρ)); for p_x>0 the conditional density is ρ_x=I_x(ρ)/p_x. Layer 1 is the sum channel, Layer 2 retains the labels and conditional states. Different instruments can have the same ensemble channel. The preceding studies address energy-moment and thermal open-system restrictions; here record transfer is an additional structure.

\[\sum_x|p_x^V-p_x^W|\le\sqrt{1+\varepsilon}-\sqrt{1-\varepsilon}\]

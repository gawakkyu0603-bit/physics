OSTQD–SK/JT: mathematical introduction for STEM readers

Starting from density matrices and thermal correlations, follow the transition from finite channels to a conditional two-dimensional gravitational model.

TL;DR

Thermal eigenoperator jumps may exchange energy yet commute with the clock-time generator at fixed temperature. An evolving bath requires additional integrability and tracking controls. The JT calculation solves frozen-coefficient non-Gaussian cumulants and compares fixed-drift equilibrium models. The free chiral horizon analysis separates non-Hadamard point splitting, raw detector convergence, and positive-type extensions. The channel and scattering benchmarks have distinct hypotheses and endpoints.

Assumptions: extend rather than replace OSTQD

Use units with ħ=k_B=1. The preceding finite-dimensional OSTQD paper assigned each observer an accessible algebra, Hamiltonian H and GKSL generator. Its exact first- and second-moment immunity forced commuting jumps. This continuation changes the assumptions to thermal reduced systems with energy exchange; the full interacting system may still conserve energy. The variable s is auxiliary dissipation, not another measured physical time. The calculations do not derive s(t) from gravity.

Fixed-temperature covariance and time-dependent obstructions

The GKSL dissipator acts as L(ρ)=Σ_k D[L_k](ρ). Suppose [H,L_k]=−ω_k L_k. Since L_k†L_k commutes with H, every term in the dissipator commutes with ad_H. Consequently e^(−it ad_H) and e^(sL) commute. The Heisenberg energy drift and carré du champ follow by expanding the commutators.

\[\mathcal L^\dagger(H)=-\sum_k\omega_kL_k^\dagger L_k,\qquad \Gamma_{\mathcal L}(H,H)=\sum_k\omega_k^2L_k^\dagger L_k\]

For the pair of lowering and raising jumps, detailed balance gives a stationary Gibbs state σ. With S=σ^(1/2) the square-root intertwining is L(SXS)=S L†(X) S. Thus the Hermitian relative density B=S⁻¹ρS⁻¹−I evolves by the dual generator. For χ²=Tr(σ B²), its derivative is minus the sum of two nonnegative weighted squared commutators. This is a thermal monotone, unlike purity.

\[\frac{d}{ds}\chi^2_\beta(\rho)=-\gamma_\downarrow\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma[L,B]^\dagger[L,B])-\gamma_\uparrow\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma[L^\dagger,B]^\dagger[L^\dagger,B])\le0\]

The unprojected two-level non-secular Redfield matrix has determinant −(γ_down−γ_up)²/4. The finite-temperature unequal-rate case therefore fails the positivity test. A consistent secular or suitable unified CPTP generator is a different model.

The initial decay of the quantum regression autocorrelation relative to the free-subsystem unitary correlation is controlled by the Dirichlet form; vanishing requires commutation with both jumps under faithfulness and positive rates. Interacting total-system correlations may nevertheless agree exactly with Markov regression. Thus the criterion is not a universal obstruction to a unitary dilation.

With K fixed, a two-time PDE ∂_tρ=Kρ, ∂_sρ=L(t)ρ is flat only if ∂_tL+[L,K]=0. If the generators are instantaneously covariant, the rectangle difference satisfies the exact trace-norm bound below. Tracking an instantaneous Gibbs state additionally requires a relaxation gap; the derivative of relative entropy to a moving reference contains an indefinite term.

\[\|(\Phi_{\rm top}-\Phi_{\rm bot})(\rho)\|_1\le\Delta s\,\Delta t\,\sup_{\tau}\|\partial_\tau\mathcal L(\tau)\|_{1\to1}\]

Uniform contraction M exp[−λ(t−v)] on traceless Hermitians gives a Duhamel–Gronwall bound on the tracking error by the decaying initial discrepancy plus M/λ times the supremum of the Gibbs-state speed. \[K_n(u)=(-1)^n\frac{n!}{n-1}4\pi^2 C T(u)^{n+1}(1-e^{-(n-1)ku}),\quad n\ge2\]

The n=2 result is the Gaussian variance. A compound-Poisson process with rate αT and exponentially distributed jumps of mean T represents the frozen-coefficient driver, not microscopic literal jumps. The independent increments belong to the driver, not the filtered energy. At u_CT=1, the recorded skewness is −0.1910 and the excess kurtosis +0.0603. State restrictions and selective instruments

At fixed s the exterior marginal is invariant, so cross-perspective agreement on the specified exterior observables follows for the conditional separated construction. When s advances in physical time, the Markov regression correlation has an extra e^(−ΓΔs/2) factor relative to the free subsystem (0.60653 at ΓΔs=1).

For number-projective outcomes, the inertial two-mode energy is ω[(2n+1)cosh(2r)−1]≥2ω sinh²r. But phase-unitary unravelings of complete dephasing have branches with arbitrarily small excitation. The noncommuting vacuum and exterior-number projectors cannot have a joint projective measurement over all states; this does not exclude common zero eigenstates in dimension at least three. Non-selective no-signaling preserves the remote marginal; conditional steering is allowed in standard quantum mechanics. Forbidding it is an additional design postulate, not a consequence.
